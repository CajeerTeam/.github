# Principles

## Autonomy

Prefer systems you can understand, own, and maintain over time.  
Dependency is sometimes necessary. Unexamined dependency is not.

## Precision

Interfaces should be explicit. Copy should be short. Naming should do real work.  
Anything unclear becomes friction.

## Restraint

A public surface does not need noise to look serious.  
Keep the visual language controlled, the navigation sparse, and the messaging exact.

## Durability

Open-source work should age well.  
That means stable boundaries, honest release notes, readable documentation, and maintainable code.

## Public edge

Cajeer Team is the official contour.  
Cajeer 404 is the public edge.

The split exists to keep the signal clean: core systems stay focused, public communication stays deliberate.

# Principles

## Autonomy
Cajeer prefers independence in structure, decision-making, and product direction.

## Minimalism
Every element should justify its existence. Excess is friction.

## Precision
Clear interfaces. Clear boundaries. Clear intent.

## Selectivity
Not everything needs to be for everyone. Strong products are often built for the right few.

## Durability
Short-term noise fades. Good systems remain readable, maintainable, and useful.
